About Us

Who We Are

PulseFit is not just a fitness brand. We are a lifestyle enabler.

With years of experience in the fitness industry, we have evolved into a comprehensive retail and distribution platform, offering an extensive range of home exercise and commercial fitness products.Our expertise extends beyond product offerings o include gym membership services, catering to diverse fitness needs and aspirations.
